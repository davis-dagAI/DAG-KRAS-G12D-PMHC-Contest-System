# Archive policy

The files named in `SUBMISSION.json` and the supplement identified by its manifest are the final locally prepared submission package. The submitter reported successful submission on November 9, 2026. A portal receipt was not supplied to this archive, so that status is attributed to the submitter rather than presented as an independently verified platform transaction.

The initial public release preserves these bytes. Later corrections should be separate, dated records that name the affected source and interpretation. Experimental measurements, if released by the organizers, belong in a separately versioned result directory with their original provenance and terms. They must not overwrite predictions or turn untested candidates into negatives.

Repository maintenance may improve navigation and verification code. A material change to the archived package requires a new release and an explicit change record. Historical commits and release identifiers remain available.

The archive intentionally excludes private development history, operational credentials and identifiers, broader company research and unreleased orchestration. Public descriptions explain the contest method without presenting this subset as the whole discovery platform.
